## Task

Identify the installed CNI and whether kube-proxy, eBPF, iptables, or IPVS implements Service forwarding. Save evidence in `N09.txt`; do not restart agents.

## Safety boundary

Observation only: do not patch cluster resources, restart services, or alter Talos machine configuration.

## Validation

From the repository root:

```bash
./scripts/validate.sh --task N09
./scripts/validate.sh --task N09 --json
```

The validator evaluates two independent criteria and returns `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, or `FAIL` without printing a solution. Answers and hints remain separate under `answers/`.
